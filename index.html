VALIDATED MAPPING PATHWAY

HOW A LOCATION ENTERS THE COOL MAP

Mapped information is evidence, not an instant post. Community observations stay separate from the COOL MAP until the approved checking and validation steps are complete.

1A learner or community member submits an observation through REPORT A ZONE.
2The report is screened for completeness, privacy, duplication, and pilot coverage.
3Assigned monitors may conduct a COOL SPACE CHECK when a structured site assessment is needed.
4A designated validator reviews the available evidence.
5Only validated information is added to the COOL MAP and Community Evidence Dashboard.
6The location is tracked and rechecked as conditions change.
STRUCTURED SITE ASSESSMENT

COOL SPACE CHECK

Review a selected school or community space through repeated, supervised observation. Record exposure, shade, airflow, occupancy, water, rest, accessibility, and nearby protective resources before recommending any action.

COOL SPACE CHECK is a structured field-assessment tool for assigned monitors. It is used to examine a selected location through consistent observation criteria. It is not the general community reporting channel.

DepEd Anti-Distraction Policy Compliant: This tracking workflow strictly prohibits active student personal cellphone use during class hours. Field observations are recorded onto physical paper ledgers by classroom health leaders and encoded only during non-class hours using a single, supervised shared computer terminal.

CLASSROOM-SAFE WORKFLOW

Observe first. Explain causes only after checking them.

1Repeat checks at comparable times and conditions.
2Record shade, direct sunlight, airflow, ventilation, working fans, occupancy, surface conditions, possible reflected heat or glare, water and rest access, and nearby protective resources.
3Use “possible contributing factor” for suspected roof or surface reflection until evidence supports it.
4After an intervention, return and recheck whether protection improved.
COMMUNITY-INITIATED REPORTING

REPORT A ZONE

Learners and community members may submit a time- and location-specific observation about a possible heat-exposure concern, protective resource, changing route condition, or service gap for checking and validation.

REPORT A ZONE is the community reporting channel of COOL-LECTIVE. It allows a user to identify a condition that may require further checking. A submitted report is not automatically treated as verified evidence and will not enter the Evidence Dashboard until it passes the approved checking and validation process.
