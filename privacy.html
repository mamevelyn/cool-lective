Privacy

PROJECT COOL-LECTIVE minimizes personal data collection. It does not require an account and does not ask for learner names, exact home addresses, or a medical diagnosis.

Device data

Community observation records and COOL POINTS progress are stored in the browser on the user’s device in this release and are not automatically uploaded to a shared server.

Extra Care Mode

Extra Care guidance profiles are temporary and are not saved. Choosing a breathing, heart/blood-pressure/circulation, medication-related, or general profile does not establish or record a diagnosis.

Official heat information

The app links to the public DOST-PAGASA iHeatMAP. DOST-PAGASA remains the official source of heat-index guidance.

Community observations

Project teams should record only publicly appropriate, non-sensitive information. Suspected causes such as reflected heat should be marked as possible contributing factors until validated.

Future services

If accounts, a shared database, analytics, notifications, or precise location are added later, this privacy notice and Google Play Data Safety declarations must be updated before release.
